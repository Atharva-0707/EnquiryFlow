MEAN Stack Architecture

Centralized Customer Communications

EnquiryFlow provides your team with a structured, reliable pipeline to capture customer questions, assign relevant categories, schedule follow-ups, and track lifecycle transitions until resolution.

Instant Intake: Direct capture of customer contact information, requirements, and custom notes.
Structured Classification: Categorize requests accurately to route them to the right team or response queue.
Full Lifecycle Visibility: Monitor progress from initial contact to follow-up date and final conversion outcome.
Real-time API synchronization with MongoDB database via Express REST API
Standard Operating Procedure

Enquiry Lifecycle Workflow

4 Key Stages

A standardized, disciplined progression for handling every customer touchpoint consistently.

Phase 01

New Enquiry Intake

Capture customer contact details, inquiry type, classification category, and primary message.

Phase 02

Scheduled Follow-up

Assign target follow-up dates to ensure timely communication and maintain customer responsiveness.

Phase 03

Status Progression

Update status states (New, In Progress, Resolved) and log ongoing customer interaction feedback.

Phase 04

Conversion & Resolution

Mark inquiry as successfully converted into a business relationship or documented as closed.
